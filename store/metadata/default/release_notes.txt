New app icon

A brand-new icon by The Iconfactory. The previous one is still available in Settings.


Offline browsing

The most requested feature: your documents are now available without a connection. Documents you open are kept on your device with their notes, file metadata and thumbnails, and open straight away next time.

Choose "Entire library" to keep every saved view and every PDF on the device, downloaded in the background, also while the app is closed. Downloads stay on Wi-Fi unless you turn on "Sync over cellular".

A new Offline & Sync screen shows sync progress, what failed and why, data use, and how much space offline data takes up.


Keep offline

Pin single documents or whole saved views so they are always available offline. Each document in a list now shows whether it is online only, downloaded, or kept offline.
