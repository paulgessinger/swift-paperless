New app icon

A new icon by The Iconfactory. The previous one is still available in Settings.


Offline browsing

Documents you open are kept on your device, with their notes and thumbnails, so
they open without a connection. Choose "Entire library" to keep everything
offline, downloaded in the background. A new Offline & Sync screen shows
progress and storage use.


Keep offline

Pin single documents or whole saved views so they are always available
offline.
