# Privacy

Swift Paperless talks to your Paperless-ngx server. It has no account system,
no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.

## Your server

Your login details, your documents and anything you upload, including through
the share extension and the Upload Document shortcut, only ever go to the
Paperless-ngx server you set up in the app. You are responsible for running
that server, or for trusting whoever runs it.

## Anonymous usage statistics

From version 1.11, you can choose to share anonymous usage statistics. This is
off unless you turn it on, and you can change it at any time in
**Settings → Anonymous usage**. The reports contain no documents, no server
address and no identifier, and the server keeps only totals. What is sent, and
how it is handled, is described in detail on
[Anonymous usage statistics](telemetry.md).

## Other connections

- **Release notes and this page** are loaded from GitHub. GitHub sees your IP
  address for these requests. Nothing else is sent.
- **Tips** are handled by Apple through the App Store. I do not
  see your payment details.
- **Crash reports** reach me only if you have turned on sharing
  with app developers in your device's settings. This is Apple's mechanism,
  and Apple handles the data.
